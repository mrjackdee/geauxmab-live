  • Business education
  • Financial wellness

Social Action

We organize service and awareness initiatives around health, civic engagement, families, and the issues affecting our neighbors.

Youth development

Sigma Beta Club

The Omicron Sigma Sigma Beta Club gives young men positive mentorship, academic encouragement, leadership practice, and a brotherhood of support. The goal is not simply to prepare participants for the next step, but to help them become principled leaders wherever they go.
