Our story

Rooted in legacy. Built for progress.

For more than nine decades, Omicron Sigma has carried the ideals of Phi Beta Sigma into the communities of North Texas.

01

Who we are

The Progressive chapter.

Omicron Sigma is a graduate chapter of Phi Beta Sigma Fraternity, Incorporated, serving brothers and communities across North Dallas, Denton, and Collin counties.

Our chapter is part of the Gulf Coast Region and the proud Texas family of Sigma. We develop men, build partnerships, and deliver programs grounded in brotherhood, scholarship, and service.

The fraternity

An inclusive vision since 1914.

Phi Beta Sigma Fraternity, Inc. was founded at Howard University on January 9, 1914, by A. Langston Taylor, Leonard F. Morse, and Charles I. Brown. They envisioned an organization that would be part of the community, not apart from it.

That “inclusive we” remains central to our work and to our constitutionally bound relationship with Zeta Phi Beta Sorority, Incorporated.

Our chapter history

A legacy measured in action.

From chartering to reactivation to continued impact, each generation has accepted responsibility for carrying the chapter forward.

1933

Chapter chartered

Omicron Sigma is established on December 1 by Bros. E.A. Burson, C.C. Tedford, C.A. Walton, Taft Wilson, and A.B. Henderson.

2006

The return

Eleven Sigma men from North Dallas, Denton, and Collin counties reactivate the chapter after a four-year hiatus.

2007

Service recognized

The Omicron Sigma Claus toy drive earns the Lone Star Graduate Chapter Social Action Program of the Year Award.

Today

Progress continues

The chapter develops leaders, supports collegiate chapters, and strengthens communities throughout the Dallas-Fort Worth area.
