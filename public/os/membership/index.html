Why Sigma

Individual talent. Collective strength.

Phi Beta Sigma nurtures individuality while creating broader opportunities for brotherhood, scholarship, service, leadership, and professional growth.

  • Personal and leadership growth
  • A disciplined approach to organizational planning
  • Progressive, innovative community programs
  • Professional relationships and lifelong brotherhood
  • Meaningful opportunities to serve
  • First-class social and cultural experiences

What we seek

Men prepared to carry the ideals forward.

Membership is about alignment with the values and responsibilities of the fraternity, not simply participation in social activities.

Common questions

Before you inquire.

What is graduate chapter membership?

Graduate chapter membership is the alumni pathway for qualified men who have earned a bachelor’s degree or higher and meet the fraternity’s requirements.

Can an existing Sigma transfer into Omicron Sigma?

Yes. Complete the chapter’s transfer or reinstatement request so the membership team can review your information and guide the next steps.

Where does the chapter serve?

Omicron Sigma’s service area includes North Dallas, Denton, and Collin counties, with activity across the greater Dallas-Fort Worth region.

Does submitting an inquiry guarantee membership?

No. An inquiry begins a conversation. Membership is governed by the policies, eligibility requirements, and official processes of Phi Beta Sigma Fraternity, Inc.
